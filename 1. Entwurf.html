1. Player Skills & Attributes

[ Replace with your custom Image/Graphic: Attributes Overview ]
⚡ The 100 to 110 Power-Spike:
Attributes do not just scale linearly. When a skill passes 100 points up to 110, your player gains a massive performance boost! Note that Aim and Awareness have very little effect below 90, while Gamesense, Teamplay, and Quickness are worth training at any level.

Gunfight Skills:

  • 🎯 Aim: Decides if you hit. Higher Aim directly increases your overall hit accuracy and headshot rate.
  • 🔫 Handling: Steadys weapon recoil during spray fire, preventing loss of headshot chance over longer bursts.
  • ⚡ Quickness: Provides a first-shot accuracy bonus (Q-Bonus) and determines rotation speed inside your view cone.
    (See Turn Speeds Module).
  • 👁️ Awareness: Determines rotation speed when snapping to targets outside your view cone and expands your Flank Zone.
    (See Flank Module).
  • 🛡️ Determination: Prevents oversteering/overshooting when snapping to targets at high speed, keeping aim focus stable under pressure and during chaotic fights.

Tactical & Utility Skills:

  • 🧠 Gamesense (Decides WHERE you point):
    • Crosshair Placement: Automatically holds head-level with precise guns (gaining headshots) and chest-level with sprayers (preventing shots from dropping to the legs).
    • Target Reading: Recognizes if a teammate's target is already dying so bullets are not wasted.
    • Anti-Movement: Mitigates up to 70% of the headshot penalty imposed by moving enemies.
  • 🤝 Teamplay (Mitigates Stacking Penalties):
    • Stacking Penalty Mitigation: Players grouped together shoot worse (especially at long range). High Teamplay reduces this penalty by up to 30%.
    • Focus Fire: Restores up to 50% precision when two players focus the same target simultaneously.
  • 🏃 Movement (How HARD you are to shoot):
    • Pure Defensive Skill: Does nothing for your own shooting; 100% goes into spoiling enemy accuracy, imposing up to 10% extra miss chance on the attacker.
    • Micro-Movements: Governs subtle fidgeting and weight shifting at corners, making the player a harder target and suppressing incoming headshots.

The Quickness Bonus (Q-Bonus) by Weapon:

When Quickness exceeds 90, your first shot receives a direct hit-chance bonus based on weapon type:

  • AWP: +6.0%
  • Desert Eagle: +4.3%
  • P2000 / Dual Berettas: +3.5% to +3.7%
  • AK-47 / M4A1: +2.2% to +2.6%
  • MP5: +1.5%

3. Turn Speeds & The 1-Pixel FOV Exploit

[ Replace with your custom Image/Graphic: Anti-Flash Wall Position Diagram ]

Tactical Execution: Anti-Flash Wall Peek

When holding an angle, players often look into a nearby wall or corner to avoid getting blinded by incoming flashbangs. To snap back onto the enemy at maximum speed, position your Vision Cone boundary so that the enemy doorway/chokepoint lies exactly 1 Pixel outside your Cone.

Why this works:
Because the enemy steps out 1 Pixel outside your active field of view, the game immediately triggers your Out of FOV Speed + Fast Flick bonus—which is significantly faster than standard In FOV rotation!

Understanding the Flank Cone:

The Effective Flank Cone is calculated as a base 45° angle + Awareness bonus (up to +30° additional width).

  • The Flank Advantage: Attacking an enemy from inside their Flank Zone gives you a 50ms faster first shot.

5. Target Selection Logic

[ Replace with your custom Image/Graphic: Target Selection Flowchart Diagram ]
🚫 Shooting Disqualification Rules:
Players will never select or shoot at a target if they are blinded by a flash, defusing the bomb, or planting the bomb. Furthermore, when throwing a Flashbang, the player cannot fire at any enemy for the exact 1-Pixel trigger location on the map where the throw occurs.
(See Module 6: Tactical Tips & Tricks for full breakdown of the 1-Pixel Flash technique).

How Target Priority Works (Step-by-Step Order):

When multiple visible enemies appear, the game follows this strict decision hierarchy:

1. Target Previous Target (Target Persistence)
If the player is already shooting an enemy who is still alive and visible, they continue firing at that target to avoid unnecessary target switching.
2. Follow Teammate's Target (Focus Fire)
If no previous target exists, the player checks if teammates are already shooting a visible target.
• Chance: Driven primarily by Gamesense.
• Teamplay Bonus: Activated when exactly 2 teammates focus the same target.
• Limit: If 3 or more teammates are already firing at the same enemy, the follow chance drops to 0% to prevent the entire team from over-focusing one target.
3. Prioritize Bomb Actions
If no team focus was triggered, players prioritize high-stakes objectives: Attackers immediately target an active Defuser, while Defenders immediately target an active Planter.
4. Highest-Threat Evaluation (Gamesense & Awareness)
If no target is selected yet, the player attempts an advanced threat evaluation.
• Activation Chance: 80% Gamesense + 20% Awareness (Capped at 90% max).
• Threat Score Calculation: Evaluates weapon base threat, distance to player, defuse status, whether the enemy is looking at the player, current HP, and player Aim level.
5. Awareness Target Filter
Higher Awareness increases the chance to filter and focus solely on enemies who are actively looking directly at the player.
6. Low-HP Target Check
Higher Aim allows players to identify weakened enemies below a specific HP threshold and prioritize finishing them off.
7. Random Target Fallback & Team Spread Limit
If no prior rules apply, the player randomly selects a target from the remaining candidates.
• Team Target Spread Limit: If teammates are already targeting 4 different visible enemies, the player MUST select one of those existing targets to prevent spreading fire across 5 separate targets simultaneously.

6. Tactical Tips & Tricks

1. The 1-Pixel Flash Tech (Flash-Line Utility Pop)

Because the game engine halts active target selection for the exact 1-Pixel trigger coordinate where a Flashbang is released, you can line up utility throws through narrow chokepoint gaps.

Tactical Advantage:
Placing your flash throw waypoint precisely on the 1-pixel corner allows your player to deploy utility without getting locked into a bad gunfight target selection. Enemies standing on the other side get fully blinded while your player transitions smoothly back to active firing!
